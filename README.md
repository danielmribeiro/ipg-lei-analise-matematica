# Análise Matematica (Mathematical Analysis)
Repository for the "Análise Matemática" course at Instituto Politécnico da Guarda (IPG), part of the "Licenciatura em Engenharia Informática" degree program. This repository contains exercises, resources, and materials related to the study of Mathematical Analysis.

## Course Objectives
Upon completion of this course, students should be able to:
1. Calculate limits of real functions of a real variable.
2. Analyze the continuity of real functions of a real variable.
3. Differentiate real functions of a real variable.
4. Apply derivatives to find maxima and minima and to sketch function graphs.
5. Find antiderivatives of real functions of a real variable.
6. Integrate real functions of a real variable.
7. Apply integral calculus to calculate areas.

## Program Contents
### Real Functions of a Real Variable
- Basic concepts, limits, and continuity
- Real numbers
- Functions; inverse functions; function composition
- Exponential and logarithmic functions
- Trigonometric functions and their inverses
- Basic topology concepts in ℝ
- Limits: infinite limits, limits at infinity, one-sided limits
- Asymptotes
- Continuous functions: properties and theorems

### Differential Calculus in ℝ
- Derivatives
- Rolle’s Theorem, Lagrange’s Theorem, and Cauchy’s Theorem
- Higher-order derivatives and Taylor’s Formula
- Applications: complete function analysis

### Integral Calculus in ℝ
- Basic antiderivatives
- Techniques: integration by parts and substitution
- Integration of powers of trigonometric functions
- Integration of functions reducible to rational fractions
- Riemann Integral
- Fundamental Theorem of Calculus; Integration Methods
- Applications: area calculation

## Assessment Criteria
- **Continuous Assessment**
    - The final grade is the arithmetic mean of three written tests.
    - A minimum score of 4 in each test is required.
- **Final Exam**
    - Available during regular, resit, and special exam periods.
    - A written exam will be conducted.
- **General Rules**
    - Students who wish to consider their grade from the Fundamentals of Mathematics course will have their final grade calculated as Max{0.85T + 0.15C, T}, where T is the final grade in this course and C is the Fundamentals of Mathematics grade.
    - Students scoring above 16 must take an oral exam. If they do not attend, their final grade will be 16.
    - Exams are closed-book, and calculators and mobile phones are prohibited.
